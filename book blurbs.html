The Danger of Darkness

The world has been peaceful, and the land has been prosperous. The wizards have lived lives of ease, only having to provide for the villages occasionally. But one day, their peaceful lives are disrupted by the return of the dark wizard and his dragon Darksmoke. They must find a way to protect their lands and defeat the dark wizard before he lays waste to their lands in his hunt for power and revenge.

Ara and the Dragons

For those of you with adventurous spirits who love the thrill of a good book about Dragons, this is the book for you! Fourteen-year-old Ara is the daughter of millionaires. Her life so far has been endless boring celebrations, being told what dress she should wear and how her hair should look. Her one joy that keeps her from going crazy is books. She loves to read about dragons, hoping to find one someday. She is invited to a secret battle and survival academy because of her interest in archery, and she accepts. After months of training, she is badly injured and is forced to quit. Her parents decide to send her to a finishing school, and away Ara goes. There is an accident, and she is rescued by dragons. They tell her that she bears the mark of the dragon riders, and that only she can save them from ceasing to exist.
